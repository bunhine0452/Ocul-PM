Shortcuts

Last updated 2026-10-28

⌘ numbers come from the sidebar order automatically — what each screen does is in the Screen Tour.

Anywhere

KeyAction
⌘KCommand palette — navigate, plus search entry, plan, discussion, and doc titles
⌘1–⌘8Jump to a screen, in sidebar order (⌘1 Today · ⌘2 Journal · ⌘3 Changes · ⌘4 Planner · ⌘5 Search · ⌘6 Skills & Rules · ⌘7 Discussions · ⌘8 Branch)
⌘[ / ⌘]Screen back / forward — like a browser, back to what you were just reading
⌘PSwitch project
⌘\Agent panel
⌘,Settings

KeyAction

⌘WClose tab
⇧⌘NNew window

The in-app terminal, editor and Claude Code screen — and their keys — were removed on 2026-10-28. Your agent runs in your own terminal; the planner's ▶Run and Today's "Resume with this context" put the prompt on the clipboard.
